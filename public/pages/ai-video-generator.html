AI Video Generator

Free plan: 3 videos a day · Text-to-video and image-to-video · No installation

The AI Video Generator turns a short description into a moving video clip — or brings a still image to life. Describe a scene ("rain on a Karachi street at night, rickshaws passing, reflections on wet roads") or upload a photo and describe the motion, and the generator produces a short clip you can download for Shorts, Reels, TikTok or a longer edit.

Two ways to make a video

Text-to-video: write the scene and the movement. Keep it to one clear action per clip — "a puppy runs across a garden toward the camera" beats three actions competing in five seconds.

Image-to-video: start from a still image — one you generated or your own photo — and describe how it should move. This is the reliable way to keep a character or product looking the same across a whole story: lock the picture first, then animate it.

How it works

  1. Write the scene — subject, one action, setting, mood.
  2. Add the camera — "slow push-in", "gentle pan", or keep it still for dialogue shots.
  3. Generate — video takes longer than an image; clips are produced by a job system, so you can watch the progress instead of staring at a frozen page.
  4. Download and edit — stitch clips together in any editor, add voiceover or music.

What people make with it

  • Short story scenes and serial episodes for YouTube and TikTok
  • Product clips for shops and marketplace listings
  • Animated photos for birthdays, tributes and family videos
  • Background loops for streams, lessons and presentations

Tips that save generations

One action per clip, one camera move per clip, and short clips joined in editing — this is the workflow every serious AI-video creator ends up with, and it is why our AI video prompts guide is built around it. For stories, keep a reference image of each character and reuse it in every scene; consistency is what separates a film from a slideshow. Honest limits

Free-plan clips are short (a few seconds each) and generated with free and open models such as Wan and CogVideoX-class systems; long, flawless, cinema-grade shots are beyond what any free generator promises today. Generation can take a few minutes when the free providers are busy. Daily limits reset each morning, and rewarded ads add bonus generations.

Frequently asked questions

How long are the videos?

A few seconds per generation — creators join several clips in an editor to build full scenes and stories.

Can I animate my own photo?

Yes — image-to-video accepts your photo and a motion description. Uploads are kept temporarily (maximum 24 hours) for processing and are never used to train models.

Is it really free?

Yes — 3 videos a day on the free plan, no credit card. Paid plans raise the limits and priority.
