9th, 10th, 11th & 12th Class Date Sheet 2027: All Pakistan Boards in One Place

October 23, 2026 · 8 min read

Every year, millions of Pakistani students search for the same thing: the date sheet for their class and board. And every year, the search begins months before the boards publish anything — which is why this page starts with the honest fact most websites will not tell you: the date sheets for the 2027 annual examinations have not been released yet. Any site showing you a complete "2027 date sheet" today is displaying last year's paper pattern or a guess dressed up as fact. What this guide gives you instead is what you actually need: when each board normally releases its date sheet, the exact step-by-step method to download yours the moment it appears, and the complete list of boards in one place so you are checking the right website on the right day.

When do the boards actually release the date sheet?

Pakistan's boards of intermediate and secondary education follow a consistent yearly rhythm. Matric (9th and 10th class) annual exams are normally held in March, and boards typically publish the matric date sheet roughly one to two months before the first paper — which puts the expected release window for the 2027 matric date sheet in January to February 2027. Intermediate (11th and 12th class) annual exams normally follow in April and May, with the inter date sheet usually appearing around March 2027, again a month or two ahead of the papers. Individual boards occasionally shift by a few weeks — Ramadan's timing, weather disruptions in northern areas, and administrative changes have all moved schedules in past years — so treat these windows as the pattern, not a promise. The date sheet is official only on the day your board posts it on its own website.

All the boards: one complete list

Pakistan's students are covered by more than two dozen boards, and checking the wrong one is the most common reason students miss announcements. In Punjab: BISE Lahore, BISE Rawalpindi, BISE Gujranwala, BISE Faisalabad, BISE Multan, BISE Sargodha, BISE DG Khan, BISE Bahawalpur, and BISE Sahiwal. In Sindh: the Karachi boards (the Board of Secondary Education Karachi for matric and the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi for inter), BISE Hyderabad, BISE Sukkur, and BISE Larkana. In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: BISE Peshawar, BISE Swat, BISE Abbottabad, BISE Kohat, BISE Mardan, BISE Bannu, and BISE Dera Ismail Khan. In Balochistan: BISE Quetta, which covers the whole province. At the federal level, the Federal Board (FBISE), Islamabad covers federal government institutions, cantonment schools, and many overseas Pakistani schools. For Azad Jammu and Kashmir, that is BISE Mirpur. Find your board in this list and bookmark its official website now, in October — not in the February rush, when board servers slow down under the traffic of every student in the province checking at once.

How to download your date sheet: step by step

Step 1: Open your board's official website — the addresses are the board name followed by the .edu.pk or .pk domain in almost every case; type it carefully, because copycat sites with similar names exist mainly to show you ads. Step 2: look for the section labelled "Date Sheet," "Downloads," or "Notifications" on the homepage — boards post the date sheet as a PDF in one of these three places. Step 3: select your examination exactly: class (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th), session (Annual 2027), and group (Science, Humanities/Arts, or Commerce), because boards often upload separate files for each. Step 4: download the PDF and save it in two places — your phone and a printed copy for your study wall. Step 5: check the PDF against your subjects one by one. Confirm every subject you registered for appears, with the group and paper code matching your roll number slip. If a subject is missing or doubled, contact your school or the board immediately — errors are correctable before the exams and a nightmare during them.

Date sheet, roll number slip, and result: do not confuse them

Three documents, three different dates. The date sheet announces when each paper will be held, and it arrives first. The roll number slip — your personal admit card with your roll number, photograph, and exam centre — follows later, usually a few weeks before the exams; regular students receive it through their school, while private candidates download it from the board website or receive it by post. The result comes months after the last paper. Students lose marks every year by preparing from a date sheet for the wrong session, or by arriving at the centre named in a friend's slip instead of their own. Treat the three as separate checkpoints and verify each one when its turn comes.

How to use the waiting time well

The months before the date sheet are not dead time — they are the highest-value study period of the year, because you know the syllabus even if you do not yet know the paper order. Build your revision plan around subjects, not dates: complete the full syllabus once, then rotate past papers. Boards repeat question styles heavily, so five years of past papers per subject is worth more than a guessed date sheet. If you make your own revision notes, diagrams, and mind maps, presentation matters for retention — many students now use free AI image and study-visual tools to turn plain text notes into clean visual summaries; studios like Ayaz AI Studio offer free AI image generation you can use for exactly that. When the date sheet finally drops, convert your subject-wise plan into a date-wise one in a single evening, leaving the last three days before each paper for that paper alone.

A warning about fake date sheets

Every exam season, social media fills with photographed "date sheets" that are last year's document with the year edited, screenshots from a different board, or pure inventions chasing shares. Verifying takes one minute: if the document is not on your board's official website, it is not official. Preparing to a fake schedule costs real students real papers every single year. Share only board links, not screenshots, and when your group chat circulates a date sheet before the official site has it, be the person who checks.

Frequently asked questions

When will the 10th class date sheet 2027 be released? Based on the boards' established pattern, expect it in January–February 2027, one to two months before the March annual exams. It is not out yet, and it becomes official only when your board publishes it on its website.

Will the 9th and 10th class date sheets come together? Usually the boards publish matric date sheets covering both classes in a single combined document, since the exams run together — but some boards upload them as separate files. Check both sections of your board's notifications.

How do private candidates get the date sheet and roll number slip? The date sheet is the same public document for everyone. For the roll number slip, private candidates typically download it from the board's website using their registration details, or receive it by post at the address given on their admission form.

What if my date sheet changes after release? Boards occasionally issue a revised date sheet when a paper clashes with a public holiday or an unforeseen event. Recheck the official site about a week before your first paper and again before each paper week — the latest PDF on the board's site is the one that counts.
