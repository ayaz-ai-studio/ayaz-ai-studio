About Ayaz AI Studio

Last updated: October 2026

Ayaz AI Studio is a free AI creative platform built for everyone — students, creators, small businesses, and the simply curious. It brings three creative tools together in one place: an AI chat assistant (Ask), step-by-step AI tutoring (Study Mode), and AI image and video generation (Imagine).

What you can do here

  • Chat with AI — ask questions, brainstorm ideas, get explanations, or turn on Study Mode for detailed, step-by-step tutoring answers.
  • Generate images — describe anything in words and get original AI-generated artwork, photos, and illustrations in seconds.
  • Generate videos — turn text prompts or still images into short AI video clips.
  • Talking Photo — make any photo talk with natural voiceover.
  • Build apps — describe the app or website you want and get working code you can preview and copy.

Free, forever plan

Every account gets a daily free allowance — currently 6 images, 3 videos and 50 chats per day, resetting every morning. Watching short rewarded ads earns bonus credits. There is no credit card required and no catch: the platform is powered by free and open AI models so creativity stays accessible to everyone.

Our mission

Professional creative tools have traditionally been expensive and complicated. We believe AI should lower that barrier, not raise it. Ayaz AI Studio exists so that a student in Karachi, a shopkeeper in Lahore, or a teenager anywhere in the world can create studio-quality images, videos and apps with nothing more than words.
