Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ayaz AI Studio ("we", "us") collects when you use ayaz-ai-studio.onrender.com, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you register, we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are never stored in readable form — they are protected with PBKDF2 hashing. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address from Google.

Content you create

We store the prompts you submit and your generation history (your "My Creations" notebook) so the app can show them back to you. Uploaded reference images are kept temporarily (maximum 24 hours) and are never used to train models.

Usage information

We count images, videos, chats and rewarded-ad views per day to enforce free-plan limits. We also log basic diagnostics (errors, performance) to keep the service reliable.

Preferences

Theme choice, dismissed notices and cookie-consent status are stored in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers.

2. Cookies and advertising

We use browser storage for sign-in sessions and preferences. If we display third-party ads in the future (such as Google AdSense), those ad providers may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We never share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We never use your uploads or prompts to train AI models.

4. Data retention and your rights

Account data is kept while your account exists. You can download your data or permanently delete your account — and everything tied to it — at any time from Menu → Settings → Danger zone → Delete My Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. For any other privacy request, email alising09876@gmail.com.

5. Children

Ayaz AI Studio is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The "Last updated" date above always shows the current version, and significant changes will be announced in the app.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: alising09876@gmail.com.
