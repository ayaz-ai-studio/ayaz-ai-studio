AI Image Generator

Free plan: 6 images a day · No installation · Works on any phone or computer

The AI Image Generator turns a written description into an original picture. Type what you want to see — "a small tea stall in Lahore at sunrise, steam rising from the cups" — pick a size and style, and the generator produces the image in seconds. Nothing is copied from a stock library; every result is created fresh from your words.

How it works

  1. Describe it. Write the subject, the setting and the mood in plain words. Specific beats vague: "a red bicycle leaning on a yellow wall, afternoon light" works far better than "a bicycle".
  2. Pick a size. Square for posts, portrait 9:16 for stories and phone wallpapers, landscape for banners and video thumbnails.
  3. Choose a style. Photorealistic, illustration, anime, 3D render, watercolor and more — the style word in your prompt steers the look.
  4. Generate and keep the best. Preview the result, regenerate the attempts you do not like, and download the version you keep. Your creations are saved in your notebook in the app.

What people make with it

  • Product photos for small shops and online listings
  • YouTube thumbnails and channel art
  • Portraits and profile pictures in any style
  • Social media posts, posters and greeting cards
  • Story illustrations for blogs, books and lessons

Tips for better images

Name the light. "Golden hour", "soft window light" or "neon night" changes a picture more than any other words. Give the camera a job: "close-up", "wide shot" or "view from above" frames the scene. Say what to leave out: if hands, text or crowds keep appearing wrong, describe the simpler scene — fewer elements almost always means a cleaner result. Honest limits

Free-plan images are generated with free and open AI models, so the very newest flagship quality is not always available, and busy scenes with many people can come out imperfect — hands and text inside images are the classic weak spots of every AI generator. Daily limits reset each morning, and a short rewarded ad adds bonus generations when you need more today.
