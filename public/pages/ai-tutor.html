AI Chat & Study Mode

Free plan: 50 chats a day · Step-by-step explanations · English and Urdu

Ask is the chat assistant inside Ayaz AI Studio: ask a question, brainstorm an idea, draft a message, or understand a topic. Turn on Study Mode and the same chat becomes a patient tutor that explains step by step — showing the method, not just the answer — the way a good teacher works through a problem on a board.

What Study Mode does differently

  • Steps, not just answers — each solution is broken into small, followable steps.
  • Your level — explanations adapt to school and college topics, from basic maths to physics, chemistry, biology and English.
  • Ask "why" — follow up on any step until it makes sense; the tutor never gets impatient.
  • Practice — ask for similar questions and check your attempt against the method.

Use it honestly — learn more

A tutor that does your homework for you teaches you nothing, and answers can occasionally be wrong — so Study Mode is built around hints, steps and checking. Our guide AI for students: homework help that actually teaches you shows the study routine that works: attempt first, ask for a hint, then verify with a twin problem you solve yourself.

Everyday chat uses

  • Drafting and polishing messages, emails and posts
  • Explaining news, documents and difficult paragraphs in simple words
  • Brainstorming names, ideas and outlines
  • Translating and practising English and Urdu

Honest limits

The free plan includes 50 chats a day. AI answers are usually right on school-level topics but are not guaranteed — for exams and important decisions, verify against your textbook or teacher.
