Talking Photo

Voiceover in English, Urdu, Hindi and Arabic · Works from a single photo

Talking Photo makes a still portrait speak your words. Upload a photo, type or choose what it should say, pick a voice — and the photo delivers the lines with moving lips and a natural voiceover. It is the fastest way to turn one picture into a presenter, a storyteller or a birthday greeting.

How it works

  1. Upload a clear photo — front-facing, well-lit portraits work best.
  2. Write the lines — a sentence or two at a time gives the cleanest result.
  3. Pick a voice — male and female voices in English, Urdu, Hindi and Arabic.
  4. Generate — the photo is animated with lip movements matched to the voiceover.

Good uses

  • Story narration with a consistent on-screen character
  • Lessons and explainers presented by one friendly face
  • Greetings and announcements in your own words
  • Product introductions for small businesses

Tips and honest limits

Short lines beat long speeches: generate in small pieces and join them in an editor. A photo where the face is turned far to the side, blurred or partly covered will animate poorly on any talking-photo system — pick the clearest portrait you have. Only animate photos you have the right to use, and never use someone else's face or voice to mislead: our AI avatars guide covers the consent rules plainly, and the voiceover walkthrough shows the full workflow.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages are supported?

English, Urdu, Hindi and Arabic voices are available, male and female.

Can I use my own photo?

Yes — your uploads are processed temporarily (maximum 24 hours) and never used to train models.
