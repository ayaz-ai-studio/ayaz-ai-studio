Free AI Tools in Ayaz AI Studio

Every tool is free to use — no credit card, no installation.

Ayaz AI Studio brings five creative AI tools together in one place. This page explains, in plain words, what each tool does, what it is best at, and where its honest limits are — so you can pick the right tool before you spend a single generation. Creating happens inside the app (a free account keeps your creations saved in your own notebook); everything on this page can be read without signing in.

🎨 AI Image Generator

Turn a sentence into a picture: product photos, portraits, illustrations, thumbnails and artwork in square, portrait or landscape sizes. Free plan: 6 images a day.

🎬 AI Video Generator

Turn a description — or a still image — into a short moving video clip for Shorts, Reels and stories. Free plan: 3 videos a day.

🗣️ Talking Photo

Make any photo speak with a natural voiceover in English, Urdu, Hindi or Arabic — the photo moves its lips in time with the words.

📚 AI Chat & Study Mode

Ask anything, or switch on Study Mode for patient, step-by-step tutoring explanations — maths, science, English and more. Free plan: 50 chats a day.

⚡ App Builder

Describe an app or website and get working code you can preview and copy — a fast way to prototype an idea before building it properly.

How the free plan works

Every account gets a daily allowance — currently 6 images, 3 videos and 50 chats — that resets every morning. Watching a short rewarded ad adds bonus generations. There is no credit card and no trial clock: the free plan is free forever, and paid plans simply raise the daily limits.
