📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. We also store the text prompts you submit for generation and your daily usage counts (images, videos, ad watches) to enforce free-tier limits.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate your account and provide image/video generation.
  • To enforce daily limits and award ad-earned credits.
  • To improve the service and prevent abuse.

3. What we never do

We never sell your personal data. We never store your password in plain text — passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 (SHA-512, 120,000 iterations).

4. Third-party generation providers

Your prompts are sent to third-party AI providers to generate images and videos. Those providers process the prompt text under their own privacy policies.

5. Data retention & deletion

You can permanently delete your account (and all associated data) at any time from Settings → Delete account.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@ayaz-ai-studio.com

❓ Help / FAQ

How do I generate an image?
Sign in, type a description in the Image tab, choose a size, and tap Generate. Images usually take 10–30 seconds.
How do I generate a video?
Switch to the Video tab, describe your scene, pick a duration and motion style, then tap Generate Video. Free-mode videos are AI stills with cinematic motion (3–8 seconds).
What are the daily limits?
Free accounts get 6 images and 3 videos per day. Limits reset every day.
How do I earn extra credits?
Tap "Watch Ad" and watch the full 30-second ad. Each completed ad gives +2 images and +1 video (up to 5 ads per day).
I forgot my password. What can I do?
If you can still sign in, change it from the menu → Settings. If you are locked out, contact support@ayaz-ai-studio.com.
How do I delete my account?
Open the menu → Settings → Delete account, enter your password, and confirm. This permanently removes your account and data.
Is my password safe?
Yes. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed with PBKDF2 (SHA-512, 120,000 iterations) with a unique salt per user.
