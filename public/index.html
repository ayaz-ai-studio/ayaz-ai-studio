📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Content you create: prompts you submit and generation history (your "My Creations" notebook).
  • Usage data: counts of images, videos, chats and ad views per day (to enforce free limits).
  • Preferences: theme choice, dismissed notices and cookie consent (stored in your browser).

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We never store your password in readable form (PBKDF2 hashing).
  • Uploaded images are kept temporarily (24h max) and never used for training.

Your rights

You can download your data, or permanently delete your account (and everything tied to it) anytime from Settings.

Contact

Questions? Email support@ayaz-ai-studio.com.

📋 Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

1. The service

Ayaz AI Studio provides AI chat, image and video generation on a free plan with daily limits (6 images, 3 videos, 50 chats). Limits reset daily.

2. Acceptable use

  • No illegal, hateful, or sexually explicit content.
  • No attempts to abuse, overload, or circumvent limits and security (including CAPTCHA).
  • You own the rights to content you generate, subject to the AI providers' terms.

3. Free plan & ads

Watching rewarded ads grants bonus credits. Abusing the ad system may lead to account suspension.

4. Availability

The service is provided "as is". Free hosting may cause occasional downtime or data resets.

5. Termination

We may suspend accounts that violate these terms. You may delete your account anytime.

❓ Help / FAQ

Quick answers

What is the Ask tab?

Chat with an AI assistant — ask questions, get explanations, or turn on 📚 Study Mode for detailed tutoring with step-by-step answers.

What is the Imagine tab?

Generate AI images and short videos from text prompts. Pick a trending style or upload a reference photo.

What are the free limits?

6 images, 3 videos and 50 chats per day. Watch ads for bonus credits.

Why did generation fail?

Free AI providers can be busy. Wait a moment and retry — failed attempts never consume credits.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are hashed, uploads auto-delete within 24 hours, and you can delete your account anytime.

How do I delete my account?

Menu → Settings → Danger zone → Delete My Account.

🧩 API Docs

For developers (all require sign-in except where noted)

Chat

POST /api/chat — { message, imageId?, study? } → { reply }

GET /api/chat/history — your chat history

DELETE /api/chat/history — clear history

Generation

GET /api/generate-image — ?prompt=&width=&height=&seed=

GET /api/generate-video — ?prompt=&duration=&motion=&seed=

POST /api/upload — { image: dataURL } → { id, url }

Account

POST /api/register, /api/login, /api/logout, GET /api/me

POST /api/watch-ad-complete — earn bonus credits

GET /api/my-creations — generation history

Auth: Authorization: Bearer <token> header.
